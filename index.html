About Munnin

Munnin is productivity software that helps registered users manage tasks, events, and recurring obligations. The application delivers scheduled notifications via SMS to keep users informed of their commitments — task due-date reminders, weekly digest summaries, and an annual recap of completed work.

Munnin is developed and operated by Corey Artim, an independent software developer based in Dayton, Ohio.

SMS Notification Program

Registered users opt in to receive SMS notifications by entering their mobile phone number into the application and explicitly enabling SMS notifications via a consent checkbox. Below is the SMS opt-in interface presented to users during application setup:

STOP and HELP

Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS notification. Upon receiving STOP, the Twilio platform will immediately cease sending further SMS to that number and respond with a confirmation. Users who wish to resume notifications later may reply START.

Users may request help by replying HELP to any message, or by emailing cartim@yourlegalhelp.com.
